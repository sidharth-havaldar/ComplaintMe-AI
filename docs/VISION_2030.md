# ComplaintMe AI — Vision 2030

> Inspirational, deliberately long-range. Nothing here is implemented or
> scheduled; see `ROADMAP.md` for actual plans. The compass, not the map.

---

## Where ComplaintMe AI Should Be in Five Years

By 2030, ComplaintMe AI is the **decision layer for customer dissatisfaction**
— the system an organization checks before deciding what to fix, what to
recall, whom to call, and where quality is drifting.

The complaint form disappears entirely. Complaints arrive from wherever
customers already are — email, chat, voice transcripts, app reviews, social
posts, partner systems — and every one of them leaves the pipeline as
intelligence, never as a ticket in a queue.

The five-minute promise of today's `PROJECT_STATE.md` becomes the product
itself: any executive, on any morning, understands the state of customer
pain across their organization in under five minutes.

## How Cortexa Evolves

Cortexa grows along the axis it was born on — each layer consuming the one
below and emitting something shorter and more actionable (Principle #3):

1. **Understanding** matures from heuristics to best-in-class language
   models, multilingual and multimodal (text, voice, images of the broken
   product), still behind the same provider contract.
2. **Decision Intelligence** becomes organization-aware: recommendations in
   the company's own vocabulary of teams, playbooks, and thresholds.
3. **Complaint Intelligence** moves from lexical similarity to semantic
   memory — Cortexa remembers every complaint an organization has ever seen
   and recognizes a recurrence across phrasing, language, and channel.
4. **Predictive Intelligence** answers "what happens next": escalation
   probability, churn risk, projected complaint velocity, and early-warning
   signals that fire before a defect trends publicly.
5. **The Learning Engine** closes the loop: Cortexa knows which of its risk
   calls proved right and which recommendations were acted on, and gets
   measurably better every quarter — per organization.
6. **Executive Intelligence** is the visible surface: not dashboards of
   charts, but briefings — "three things need a decision this week, and
   here's the evidence."

Throughout, the founding constraints hold: providers stay swappable, privacy
stays first (organization data trains only that organization's intelligence),
and every feature still has to pass the Golden Question.

## How Enterprises Use It

- **The quality leader** at a manufacturer sees a component-level defect
  pattern eleven days after the first complaint, not eleven weeks — with an
  evidence trail ready for engineering.
- **The COO** reads a Monday briefing: risk portfolio, trend deltas, the two
  patterns that crossed a threshold, and the recommended owner for each.
- **The support director** stops triaging: complaints arrive pre-understood,
  pre-routed, and pre-prioritized; humans handle judgment and empathy.
- **The compliance officer** exports the full decision trail — what Cortexa
  flagged, when, who acted — as audit evidence.
- **The product team** treats complaint intelligence as a roadmap input with
  the same weight as analytics and revenue data.

Deployment fits the enterprise: multi-tenant SaaS by default, private
deployments (own cloud, own models via the provider contract) where data
residency demands it.

## Competitive Positioning

The market will remain crowded with systems of **record** — CRMs, help
desks, ticketing platforms — many with AI features bolted on: summarize this
ticket, draft this reply. Those features make ticket-handling faster.

ComplaintMe AI competes on a different axis: it is a system of **decision**.
The unit of value is not a closed ticket but a better decision — a recall
triggered earlier, a defect caught sooner, a churn wave prevented.

Defensible ground:

- **The intelligence loop.** Years of complaint → outcome → feedback data
  per organization makes Cortexa's judgment compounding and hard to copy.
- **Provider independence.** Model vendors change; the contract doesn't.
  ComplaintMe AI rides every model improvement without re-architecture.
- **The premium bar.** Decision-makers actually open it — a product that
  feels like Linear or Stripe in a category that feels like a government
  portal.

**Realistic ambition:** ComplaintMe AI does not need to replace the
enterprise's CRM to win. It needs to be the intelligence those systems feed
— and by 2030, the phrase "check Cortexa first" should be how organizations
begin any conversation about customer pain.
