# ComplaintMe AI

> **Tell us what happened. We'll understand the rest.**

ComplaintMe AI is an AI-powered Complaint Intelligence Platform that transforms unstructured complaints into structured, actionable insights.

Instead of asking users to fill out long complaint forms, ComplaintMe AI allows them to describe their issue naturally. Our AI engine, **Cortexa**, analyzes the complaint, extracts meaningful information, and generates intelligence that can help users, organizations, and enterprises understand problems faster.

---

## Vision

To become the intelligence layer between customer voices and organizations.

---

## Mission

Transform every complaint into actionable intelligence using AI.

---

# The Problem

Customer complaints today are scattered across:

- Customer Support
- Emails
- Social Media
- Reviews
- Government Portals
- Contact Forms

Organizations receive thousands of complaints every day, but most remain unstructured and difficult to analyze.

---

# Our Solution

ComplaintMe AI converts free-text complaints into structured intelligence.

Example

User writes:

> "My internet has been down for four days and customer support keeps closing my tickets."

Cortexa automatically extracts:

- Category
- Company
- Priority
- Sentiment
- Expected Outcome
- Actual Outcome
- Summary
- Similar Complaints

No manual classification required.

---

# Cortexa Engine

Cortexa is the a modular AI intelligence framework that orchestrates multiple AI capabilities to analyze and structure customer complaints, powering ComplaintMe.

It is designed as a modular AI pipeline capable of understanding customer voice across multiple domains.

Current Modules:

- Complaint Classification
- Sentiment Analysis
- Entity Recognition
- Priority Detection
- Expectation Extraction
- Complaint Summarization

Future Modules:

- Review Intelligence
- Survey Intelligence
- Employee Feedback Analysis
- Customer Support Intelligence
- Voice Complaint Analysis
